Waiting for the watches

Nothing has arrived from a watch yet. To show both watches here over their USB cables:

  1. Plug both watches into this computer.
  2. Stop the bridge (Ctrl-C in its terminal): loading a watch needs its USB port. Load each watch in debug mode, with P its port (mpremote devs lists them): python3 tools/deploy.py --port P --debug A for one and python3 tools/deploy.py --port P --debug B for the other.
  3. Start the bridge again with python3 tools/debug_server.py --serial and open http://localhost:8765/local.html. Each screen shows up here a few seconds after its watch starts.

Over Wi-Fi instead: save the Wi-Fi name and password once with python3 tools/wifi_setup.py, load the watches with --debug A --wifi and --debug B --wifi, and keep both on this computer's Wi-Fi (a network with one access point works best).

No watches at hand? Stop the bridge and start it again with python3 tools/debug_server.py --demo --serial to see two pretend watches play.
